Why Cost per Lead is the wrong metric to steer on

Most lead generation campaigns are optimised for one number: cost per lead, as low as possible. The problem is that a low CPL says nothing about the quality of those leads. Ten cheap, unqualified leads ultimately cost you more time and money than two expensive, well-qualified leads that actually convert.

Max. CPL = deal value × gross margin% × (1 − desired profit buffer%) × conversion rate% The CPL you can afford depends on what a customer is worth to you, not on what competitors pay.

At Momenti, every lead generation campaign is built on this calculation: what's the deal value, what's the lead-to-customer conversion rate, and what CPL is still profitable at that point? Only then is it optimised at the platform level.

Lead generation via Meta Ads & Google Ads

Which platform works best depends on where your audience is in the buying journey. Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) is strong for lead generation that works on interrupted attention - people not actively searching, but who can be persuaded by the right message at the right moment. Google Ads is strong when there's already active search intent.

For recruitment lead generation, targeting on job title, persona and vacancy urgency is more effective than broad demographic targeting - a specialism few agencies are genuinely good at.
