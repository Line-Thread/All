Why outsource Google Ads?

Google Ads, also known as SEA (Search Engine Advertising) or, under its old name, Google AdWords, captures demand the moment it arises: someone searches, you're there. But the platform is built to spend budget, not to maximise your profit. Performance Max puts budget into what converts easily, not what delivers the most. Without sharp setup, you're paying for revenue that would have come anyway.

That's why Momenti steers on margin per product and category. Which products deserve budget, which earn it back, and which are better paused? Those choices make the difference between turning revenue and making profit.

Frequently asked questions

What does outsourcing Google Ads cost?

A retainer for ongoing Google Ads management typically starts around €750 per month, for accounts with a media budget up to €5,000 on one platform. With higher budgets or more complexity (Google Ads and Meta combined, Shopping, Performance Max or multiple campaign goals), rates are usually between €1,200 and €2,500 per month. There's also a one-off setup fee for onboarding or taking over the account, typically around €750 depending on complexity.

Am I tied to a contract?

No. A two-week trial period in which either side can stop at no cost, then a one calendar month notice period. No annual contracts.

Does Performance Max actually work for my webshop?

Often, yes, provided it's set up well. Performance Max needs strong product data and a structure that stops all the budget going to low-margin bestsellers. That's exactly what the account analysis looks at.
