2. What data do we collect?

Via the contact form on the website, you can leave the following data:

  • Name
  • Company name
  • Email address
  • Message

This data is only provided when you fill in and submit the form.

3. What do we use your data for?

The submitted data is used solely to respond to your message and, where relevant, to start a conversation or collaboration. We do not use your data for marketing, newsletters or profiling without your explicit consent.

4. Legal basis

The processing of your data is based on legitimate interest - handling business contact requests. We do not process more data than strictly necessary for this purpose.

5. Retention period

We do not keep your data longer than necessary for the purpose for which it was provided, or as long as needed to comply with legal obligations. Contact messages are generally kept for no longer than two years.

6. Third parties and processors

To process the contact form, we use Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party service that forwards form submissions to our mailbox. For this purpose, Formspree temporarily processes the submitted data on its servers. See Formspree's privacy policy for more information.

The website uses Google Fonts - fonts that are loaded from Google's servers. This may transmit your IP address to Google. See Google's privacy policy for more information.

We do not share your data with other third parties, unless legally required to do so.

7. Cookies

This website does not place tracking or analytics cookies. Formspree and Google Fonts may use technical cookies or similar technologies as part of their service. We have no direct control over this.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access - you can request which data we process about you.
  • Right to rectification - you can have inaccurate data corrected.
  • Right to erasure - you can request that your data be deleted.
  • Right to restriction - you can request that processing be restricted.
  • Right to object - you can object to the processing.

To exercise these rights, send an email to arthur@momenti.agency. We respond within 30 days.

9. Filing a complaint

Do you have a complaint about how we handle your personal data? You can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) via autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.

10. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated as required by our services or by legislation. The most current version can always be found at momenti.agency/en/privacy-policy.
