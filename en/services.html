Running less than €7,500?

It's still possible, but as a custom, scaled-down version: four new concepts per month instead of eight, optimisation every two weeks instead of weekly, and a quarterly check-in. From €749 per month.

What you need to weigh up yourself. With a smaller budget, it takes longer before an ad gets enough impressions to prove anything. Four concepts on €4,000 will give you roughly as much insight over three months as eight concepts on €8,000 in one month. It works, it's just slower — and whether that speed is worth the price difference, you know better than I do. What I can give you is the maths, so you can make that call yourself.

How we work

Four principles

Fixed monthly price

No hourly rate and no percentage of your budget. With a percentage, an agency earns more the more you spend. That's not the same interest as yours.

Limited capacity

A deliberately small number of clients at a time. You have direct contact with the person actually working in your account.

Calculated from your numbers

Margin, repeat purchases and cash flow. A campaign that looks good in the dashboard and delivers nothing to the bank account is a failed campaign.

Honest about what doesn't help

If a second channel doesn't add up, you'll hear it. If your offer is the problem and not your ads, you'll hear that too.

What counts as a new concept

A new concept is a different visual, a different angle or a different offer. A variant is the same visual with different copy or a different colour. Variants are unlimited and don't count. That line exists so you never have to think twice before asking for a small tweak.

Add-ons

What you can add

Second channel

The same management on a second ad channel. Offer, positioning and creative direction are shared, so the second channel costs less than the first. A third channel is custom and considered separately.

€900 per month

Video concepts

For anyone who films themselves or has their own videographer. You get the thinking, not the production: the hook written out word for word, the story shot by shot, on-screen text per moment, the CTA, and format with length.

€350 for 3 concepts — €500 for 5

Video feedback

After the videos have run: an analysis of hook rate and hold rate with a concrete direction for the next round. This is what a videographer can't deliver, because they never see the ad data.

€150 per round

With video concepts, the raw footage is reviewed before editing, with a go or no-go. Material that isn't going to work doesn't go live. That protects the campaign and your budget.

Boundaries

What's not included

Video filming, UGC production, photography and shoots. Landing pages and web development. Email marketing. Print materials. SEO. Organic social media. These are quoted separately or arranged through a partner.

Term
Minimum 3 months. Judging static creatives on one month of data doesn't give a usable verdict — three cycles are needed to see which direction wins. After that, cancellable monthly with one month's notice.
Delivery
You supply product imagery, logo, fonts and brand assets in usable quality. If assets are missing, delivery is pushed back, but the monthly term keeps running.
Turnaround
First batch of creatives within 10 working days of complete delivery. After that, in the first week of every month.
Feedback
One feedback round per concept, with a response within 3 working days. No response within that time counts as approval.
Unused work
Concepts not used within a month expire at the end of that month and can't be carried over.
Rights
Usage rights to delivered creatives transfer upon payment of the relevant monthly invoice.
Invoicing
In advance, monthly, payment within 14 days.
Access and ownership
You remain the owner of your ad account, your pixel and all your data. Momenti works with access, not ownership. On termination, access is revoked and everything stays with you.
