Growth and advice

When ads aren’t the only thing

Ads are one lever. For the rest of your growth we work by appointment and tailored to you, after an initial conversation.

Growth Marketing

We look at the whole route from first contact to repeat purchase: where new revenue comes from, what it costs and what is left after margin. Together we decide where the next euro does the most, and we test it small before making it big.

Fits whenYou are running ads, but you don’t know where to invest next.

Marketing Advice

A senior view on your marketing, without having to hire anyone. An audit of what is running now, a plan for the coming months, or a regular sparring moment with you or your team. You get an honest verdict, even if that means you should do less.

Fits whenYou want a second opinion, or you are stuck in your own marketing.

CRO Advice

Conversion rate optimisation: you get visitors, but too few enquiries or sales. With your data at hand, we look at your landing pages, product pages and checkout, and deliver a list of improvements in order of expected impact. Building is for your own developer or web agency; we provide the analysis and the advice.

Fits whenPlenty of traffic, little result.

Business Innovation

Sparring about growth beyond your current offer: a new product, a new market or a new revenue model. We run the numbers and test it before a lot of money goes in. We run a brand ourselves, so we know what an idea costs once it becomes real money.

Fits whenYou want to grow, but more of the same isn’t the answer.

Not included: web development, SEO, email marketing and photo or video production. That is quoted separately or arranged through a partner.

Creative

Creative direction

An ad is only as good as the idea behind it. We help with briefing, concept, hook and visual direction, and with feedback on what is running, based on conversion logic rather than taste.

In the core package

  • 8 new static concepts per month, in 4:5 and 9:16
  • Text iteration in week 2 on the three best-performing concepts
  • Unlimited variants of existing concepts

On top

  • Video concepts: the hook, the story and the shot list written out, for those who film themselves
  • Feedback on hook rate and hold rate after the videos have run
  • Photo and video production itself isn’t included
