Why advertise on Threads now?

Threads has been available as an ad placement within the Meta ecosystem since 2025. That means you don't need to learn a separate account, platform or tool. Threads ads run through Meta Ads Manager, the same place your Instagram and Facebook campaigns already live.

The advantage of getting in early is simple: new placements typically see less competition, and therefore often more favourable cost per thousand impressions. Getting in now builds a head start while the channel is still young. But as everywhere: it only works if the setup is right and you steer on margin, not reach.

One strategy across all Meta platforms

The real value of Threads lies in how it all connects. Combine it well with Instagram and Facebook, and you're visible across every platform within Meta, from one strategy, one message and one budget logic. Your marketing stops being a collection of separate per-channel campaigns and becomes one coherent story that the same customer encounters in multiple places and moments.

That makes your approach one whole: each platform reinforces the other, your brand lands more consistently and you get more out of the same budget. Threads isn't just an extra channel here, it's the link that completes your presence within Meta.
