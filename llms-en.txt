## Core principle: optimising for margin

ROAS says nothing about what you actually keep. A ROAS of 4 on a product with a 20% margin loses
money; a ROAS of 2 on a product with a 70% margin makes money. Momenti calculates the break-even
ROAS per product group and optimises accordingly. The same principle applies to lead generation:
the maximum cost per lead follows from deal value, gross margin and conversion rate, not from a
benchmark. If the problem isn't the ads but the margin, the offer or the landing page, that's
what gets said.
